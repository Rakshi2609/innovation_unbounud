# Customer Hardship Relief & Early Financial Distress Intervention Policy (POL-HD-2026)

## Section 1: Early Distress Identification (Pre-Delinquency)
* **Clause 1.1 (Early Warning Triggers)**: Borrowers showing 2 or more consecutive months of minimum-only credit card payments, alongside cash flow compression exceeding 80.0% of net income, qualify for proactive outreach.
* **Clause 1.2 (Non-Punitive Assistance)**: Early distress flags must be handled compassionately. The automated system shall offer budgeting insights and voluntary payment restructuring without negative bureau credit reporting if the borrower participates before 60-day delinquency.

## Section 2: Approved Hardship Relief Programs
* **Clause 2.1 (Grace Period & Moratorium)**: Eligible borrowers facing temporary medical or involuntary employment interruptions may receive up to 3 months of principal repayment moratorium.
* **Clause 2.2 (Interest Rate Reduction)**: For structured workout plans, interest rates on unsecured revolving debts may be reduced to statutory baseline plus 1.5% to ensure sustainable monthly repayments.
* **Clause 2.3 (Consolidation Facility)**: Multiple unsecured lines may be aggregated into a single subsidized repayment schedule with waived late fees.
