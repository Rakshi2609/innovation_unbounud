# Customer Hardship Relief & Early Financial Distress Intervention Policy (POL-HD-2026)

**Policy ID:** POL-HD-2026
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Review Cycle:** Annual
**Owning Function:** Collections Strategy & Customer Care Operations
**Applicable To:** All retail lending products (credit cards, personal loans, consumer durable loans, BNPL, microfinance, two-wheeler and four-wheeler loans, mortgage and LAP). This policy applies to customers in early distress (pre-delinquency), active delinquency, and post-restructuring stabilization phases.

This policy operationalizes the RBI's "Framework for Compromise Settlements and Technical Write-offs", the Fair Practices Code for Lenders, and the Charter of Customer Rights. It mandates proactive, non-punitive outreach at the earliest sign of distress, defines a structured catalogue of relief measures, and sets out reporting and governance standards.

---

## Section 1: Early Distress Identification (Pre-Delinquency)

### Clause 1.1 — Early Warning Triggers
**Clause 1.1** Borrowers showing **2 or more** consecutive months of minimum-only credit card payments, alongside cash flow compression exceeding **80.0%** of net income, qualify for proactive outreach. Outreach includes automated SMS, IVR nudge, and a curated financial wellness module that explains the risk of debt spirals and available relief pathways.

### Clause 1.2 — Non-Punitive Assistance
**Clause 1.2** Early distress flags must be handled compassionately. The automated system shall offer budgeting insights, voluntary payment restructuring, and hardship program enrollment without negative bureau credit reporting if the borrower participates before **60-day delinquency**. The credit information report (CIR) shall not carry an NPA / SMA flag during this window.

### Clause 1.3 — Behavioral Signal Library
**Clause 1.3** The early-warning engine ingests the following signals, individually and in combination:
- Decline in monthly inflows of more than **25.0%** versus the trailing 6-month average.
- Increase in bounce / return transactions by **3x** versus baseline.
- Shift to alternate-payment-routes (e.g., BNPL / micro-credit originations) more than **4** in 30 days.
- Telecom-derived stress signals (drop in call plan upgrade, shift to lower-tier plan, payment delinquency on the same customer).
- Utility payment deferral pattern (electricity, water, gas) detected via Account Aggregator or partner data.

### Clause 1.4 — Outreach Cadence
**Clause 1.4** Outreach cadence escalates gently:
- Day 0–30: in-app banner and a single SMS nudge.
- Day 30–60: IVR voice call with empathy-trained bot, optional link to human advisor.
- Day 60–90: priority human call from a hardship specialist, with multilingual support.
- Day 90+: routing to collections workstream while preserving hardship program eligibility.

## Section 2: Approved Hardship Relief Programs

### Clause 2.1 — Grace Period & Moratorium
**Clause 2.1** Eligible borrowers facing temporary medical or involuntary employment interruptions may receive up to **3 months** of principal repayment moratorium. Interest continues to accrue on the outstanding principal at the contractual rate; the borrower is billed an interest-only statement during the moratorium.

### Clause 2.2 — Interest Rate Reduction
**Clause 2.2** For structured workout plans, interest rates on unsecured revolving debts may be reduced to **statutory baseline plus 1.5%** to ensure sustainable monthly repayments. The reduced rate shall hold for a minimum of **12 months** subject to sustained payment performance.

### Clause 2.3 — Consolidation Facility
**Clause 2.3** Multiple unsecured lines may be aggregated into a single subsidized repayment schedule with waived late fees. Tenor may be extended to a maximum of **60 months** for the consolidated facility, with EMI capped at **35.0%** of verified monthly income.

### Clause 2.4 — Term Extension
**Clause 2.4** For term loans (personal / consumer durable / vehicle), tenor may be extended by up to **24 months** beyond the original contracted tenor, subject to residual value adequacy and amortization discipline.

### Clause 2.5 — Step-Up EMI Schedule
**Clause 2.5** Customers expecting a verifiable income recovery within 12–24 months may opt for a step-up EMI schedule with smaller initial EMIs escalating by 10.0%–15.0% annually. The total interest cost is documented in the sanction letter addendum.

### Clause 2.6 — Partial Settlement / One-Time Settlement (OTS)
**Clause 2.6** For chronically delinquent accounts where recovery prospects are weak, the bank may offer a one-time settlement at a discount not exceeding **50.0%** of the outstanding principal plus contractual interest. Settlements above this threshold require Regional Collections Head approval and audit trail documentation.

## Section 3: Eligibility, Documentation, and Application Process

### Clause 3.1 — Eligibility Criteria
**Clause 3.1** A customer is eligible for hardship relief if they satisfy one or more of the following:
- Loss of employment (with proof — termination letter, employer attestation, or unemployment registration).
- Medical emergency (with hospital records and >30 days of absence from work).
- Business downturn for self-employed (with GST return dip, GSTN portal statement, or audited financials).
- Natural calamity declaration by district administration affecting the borrower's residence / business.
- Permanent disability reducing earning capacity (with medical board certificate).
- Death of primary earning family member (with death certificate and income dependency proof).

### Clause 3.2 — Application Channels
**Clause 3.2** Customers may apply for hardship relief through:
- Mobile banking app — dedicated "Need Help?" tile.
- Branch walk-in to the dedicated Customer Care desk.
- Toll-free multilingual helpline (24x7).
- Email to the hardship cell.
- Postal letter to the Nodal Officer.

### Clause 3.3 — Required Documentation
**Clause 3.3** Mandatory documentation scaled to the relief category:
- Income proof (latest payslip, bank statement, ITR).
- Reason-for-distress documentation per Clause 3.1.
- Recent 6 months' bank statement.
- Existing obligation schedule (auto-extracted from internal records; customer may add others).
- ID proof (Aadhaar / PAN) for KYC refresh.

### Clause 3.4 — Decision Timeline
**Clause 3.4** Hardship relief decisions must be communicated within **7 business days** of complete documentation. Incomplete applications are reverted with a checklist within **48 hours**. Approved restructuring is implemented in the next statement cycle.

## Section 4: Communication, Tone, and Customer Empathy

### Clause 4.1 — Empathy-Trained Agents
**Clause 4.1** All agents contacting customers in distress must complete annual empathy and vulnerability-sensitivity training. Quality assurance scoring on calls shall weight empathetic language and respectful tone at **30.0%** of the agent's QA score.

### Clause 4.2 — Prohibited Practices
**Clause 4.2** The bank strictly prohibits:
- Calls before 8:00 AM or after 9:00 PM.
- Calls or messages to relatives, friends, or employers about the debt (except to locate the customer with consent).
- Misrepresentation of legal consequences.
- Public humiliation or threat language.
- Persistence after explicit "do not contact" request, except to honor statutory notice requirements.

### Clause 4.3 — Plain Language
**Clause 4.3** All hardship-related communications shall be in plain language, free of legalese, and in the customer's preferred language (English plus at least one vernacular). Customers shall never be charged a fee for accessing the hardship program.

## Section 5: Bureau Reporting Discipline

### Clause 5.1 — Pre-Delinquency No Adverse Reporting
**Clause 5.1** Customers actively enrolled in the hardship program (or who have initiated contact about enrollment) within the **60-day** pre-delinquency window shall not carry adverse bureau markers. CIR updates during this window are suppressed subject to the customer's continued good-faith participation.

### Clause 5.2 — Restructured Asset Reporting
**Clause 5.2** Restructured accounts shall be reported to bureaus with the standard "restructured" asset classification as per the RBI's Master Direction on Prudential Framework. The "restructured" flag shall be removed upon **24 months** of clean repayment post-restructuring.

### Clause 5.3 — Settled Accounts
**Clause 5.3** OTS-settled accounts shall be reported as "settled" in the CIR with the date of settlement. They may be eligible for fresh credit consideration after **24 months** of clean credit history.

### Clause 5.4 — Right to Rectification
**Clause 5.4** Customers may dispute inaccurate CIR entries. The bank shall investigate and respond within **30 days**, escalating to the Credit Information Company (CIC) grievance mechanism where required.

## Section 6: Governance, Approval Authority, and Audit

### Clause 6.1 — Tiered Approval Authority
**Clause 6.1** Hardship relief sanction authority is delegated as follows:

| Relief Type                       | Approving Authority              |
|-----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| EMI moratorium up to 3 months     | Collections Manager              |
| Interest reduction (per Clause 2.2) | Regional Collections Head       |
| Consolidation / Tenor extension   | Zonal Credit Head                |
| Partial settlement / OTS          | Regional Collections Head        |
| OTS above 50.0% discount         | National Collections Committee   |

### Clause 6.2 — Monthly Hardship Council Review
**Clause 6.2** A monthly Hardship Council chaired by the Chief Risk Officer reviews hardship program metrics: enrollments, conversion to cure, dropouts, cure rate, and program profitability. Council decisions are minuted and reported to the Board Risk Management Committee quarterly.

### Clause 6.3 — Independent Audit
**Clause 6.3** Annual independent audit of the hardship program covers: program eligibility adjudication, customer communication samples, bureau reporting accuracy, and conformity with RBI's Fair Practices Code. Audit observations are remediated within **90 days**.

## Section 7: Cross-Sell Discipline During Hardship

### Clause 7.1 — No Aggressive Cross-Sell
**Clause 7.1** Customers in the hardship window shall **not** be marketed unsecured credit, premium cards, or wealth products via outbound channels. Inbound requests are honored normally with full suitability assessment.

### Clause 7.2 — Insurance Claims Assistance
**Clause 7.2** Customers with active credit protection insurance (CPI) / loan insurance may be assisted with claim filing at no charge. Insurance proceeds, when received, are applied to the outstanding loan balance per the policy terms.

## Section 8: Special Hardship Scenarios

### Clause 8.1 — Death of Borrower
**Clause 8.1** Upon intimation of a borrower's death, the account is flagged as "settled in probate". Legal heirs are contacted empathetically. Claims processing on life cover / insurance is expedited. The bank shall not initiate recovery actions against legal heirs until a clear succession certificate / legal heir certificate is presented or a court order is obtained.

### Clause 8.2 — Natural Calamity / Pandemic Events
**Clause 8.2** In the event of a state or national calamity (declared by competent authority), the bank shall offer a **6-month** blanket moratorium on all eligible retail exposures in the affected geography, in line with RBI's disaster-management directives.

### Clause 8.3 — Cyber Fraud Victims
**Clause 8.3** Customers who have been victims of authorized push-payment (APP) fraud or account takeover (per SOP-FR-2026) are eligible for expedited hardship review, including waiver of disputed charges after investigation. Loan accounts negatively impacted by fraud receive priority rehabilitation.

### Clause 8.4 — Critical Illness and Disability
**Clause 8.4** Customers certified with critical illness or permanent disability by a government-empaneled medical board are eligible for extended tenor (up to the residual working life) and interest reduction to the statutory floor, subject to documented income profile.

## Section 9: Customer Education and Financial Wellness

### Clause 9.1 — In-App Wellness Module
**Clause 9.1** A free in-app financial wellness module offers: budgeting tools, debt-snowball / debt-avalanche calculators, EMI planning aids, and educational content in 12 Indian languages. No login is required for the educational library.

### Clause 9.2 — Community Outreach
**Clause 9.2** The bank partners with recognized NGOs (registered under Section 25 / Section 8 of the Companies Act or as registered societies) to deliver financial literacy workshops in low-income and rural geographies, with a quarterly impact report.

### Clause 9.3 — Self-Service Restructuring Eligibility Check
**Clause 9.3** Customers may run a non-binding eligibility check for the hardship program via the mobile app, subject to explicit consent. The check does not affect the credit score and is purely informational.

## Section 10: Metrics, KPIs, and Reporting

### Clause 10.1 — Mandatory KPIs
**Clause 10.1** The following KPIs are tracked monthly:

| KPI                                    | Target               |
|----------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Pre-delinquency outreach coverage      | >= 95.0%             |
| Hardship enrollment within 30 days of contact | >= 60.0%       |
| Cure rate (12-month post-relief)       | >= 55.0%             |
| Customer satisfaction (CSAT)           | >= 4.2 / 5           |
| Grievance resolution within SLA        | >= 95.0%             |
| Repeat enrollment rate (rolling 12m)   | <= 12.0%             |

### Clause 10.2 — Regulatory Reporting
**Clause 10.2** Hardship program metrics are reported to the Board Risk Management Committee quarterly and to RBI in the prescribed returns as applicable. Material customer-impact events are escalated to the Customer Service Committee within **48 hours**.

### Clause 10.3 — Benchmarking
**Clause 10.3** The bank benchmarks its hardship program outcomes (cure rate, time-to-decision, customer satisfaction) against industry peers through an annual third-party study. Findings inform policy revisions.

## Section 11: Interactions with Other Policies

### Clause 11.1 — Linkage with Underwriting (POL-LEND-2026.1)
**Clause 11.1** Hardship program data feeds back into underwriting models as a low-impact positive signal when customers successfully complete the program. Repeat hardship enrollment without cure is a risk signal.

### Clause 11.2 — Linkage with Fraud SOP (SOP-FR-2026)
**Clause 11.2** Fraud-induced hardships are handled jointly with the fraud SOP to ensure legitimate customers receive immediate relief and fraud actors are pursued separately under the fraud workstream.

### Clause 11.3 — Linkage with Gig Worker Framework (POL-GIG-2026)
**Clause 11.3** Gig and informal segment customers with seasonal income volatility are eligible for income-contingent relief under the Gig Worker Framework without first triggering standard delinquency metrics.

## Section 12: Glossary

- **Hardship Program:** A structured set of relief measures offered to customers in financial distress.
- **OTS (One-Time Settlement):** A negotiated lump-sum payment to close a delinquent account at less than the full outstanding.
- **Restructuring:** Modification of loan terms (tenor, rate, EMI, moratorium) without changing the principal sanction amount.
- **CIR:** Credit Information Report maintained by bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark).
- **SMA / NPA:** Special Mention Account / Non-Performing Asset — classification per RBI's IRAC norms.
- **CPI:** Credit Protection Insurance — optional insurance cover on loan outstanding.
