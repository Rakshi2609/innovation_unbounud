# Gig Economy & Informal Worker Cashflow Underwriting Guidelines (POL-GIG-2026)

## Section 1: Non-Traditional Income & Volatility Assessment
* **Clause 1.1 (180-Day Cashflow Average)**: For gig economy contractors, freelancers, and platform workers without fixed payslips, income eligibility is determined by the 180-day rolling average of digital bank deposits.
* **Clause 1.2 (Volatility Coefficient Buffer)**: If monthly income volatility exceeds 40.0%, credit limits must be dynamically adjusted to 60.0% of the lowest quarterly income baseline to prevent overindebtedness during seasonal lulls.

## Section 2: Flexible Repayment and Micro-Liquidity Lines
* **Clause 2.1 (Income-Contingent Micro-Lines)**: Gig workers maintaining positive platform activity ratings are eligible for income-contingent liquidity lines up to ₹25,000 with flexible daily/weekly micro-deductions.
* **Clause 2.2 (Essential Expense Overdraft)**: Emergency advances specifically designated for vehicle maintenance, health emergencies, or tools of trade qualify for zero-penalty deferred repayment plans.
