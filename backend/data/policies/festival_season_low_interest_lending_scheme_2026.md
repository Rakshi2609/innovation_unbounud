# Festival Season Low Interest Lending Scheme 2026
**Category:** Lending Underwriting
**Status:** Active Institutional Policy
**Jurisdiction:** Reserve Bank of India / Commercial Lending Framework

---

## 1. Scope & Guidelines
Clause 1.1 Special Interest Relief: Borrowers applying during festive periods receive a special flat 6.5% interest rate. Clause 1.2 Processing Fee Waiver: 100% waiver on documentation fees.
