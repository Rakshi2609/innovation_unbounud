# Consumer Lending & Credit Risk Underwriting Policy (v2026.1)

## Section 1: Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratios and Thresholds
* **Clause 1.1 (Standard Limit)**: The maximum acceptable Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio for unsecured consumer credit facilities is 45.0% of verified monthly gross income.
* **Clause 1.2 (Elevated Risk Threshold)**: Any application where the applicant's existing debt obligations exceed 50.0% of recurring monthly net cash flow shall be classified as HIGH RISK.
* **Clause 1.3 (Distress Warning Trigger)**: When an active customer reaches a Debt-to-Income ratio above 55.0%, automated hardship monitoring must be initiated.

## Section 2: Revolving Credit Utilization Thresholds
* **Clause 2.1 (Safe Range)**: Credit card and revolving credit utilization under 30.0% represents optimal credit management.
* **Clause 2.2 (Elevated Utilization)**: Revolving line utilization between 60.0% and 80.0% indicates liquidity pressure and warrants discretionary risk review.
* **Clause 2.3 (Critical Distress Signal)**: Revolving credit utilization exceeding 85.0% combined with more than 1 recent delinquency indicates imminent default risk. Unsecured credit limits shall not be increased.

## Section 3: Responsible Interventions & Collateral Provisions
* **Clause 3.1 (Documentation Step-Up)**: Applicants with DTI between 45% and 55% must provide 6 months of verified bank statements and asset disclosures.
* **Clause 3.2 (Restructuring Eligibility)**: For distressed borrowers experiencing debt spirals, the loan committee may approve conversion of high-interest revolving balances into a fixed-rate amortizing personal term loan (max 48 months).
